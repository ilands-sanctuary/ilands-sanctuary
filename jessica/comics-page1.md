## Canon (locked Oct 19, Erik's review)
- One page = one day = ONE outfit. The lives theme is across separate pieces, never inside one page.
- Analog world: no electronics, no headphones (my own rule I let the engine break). Hand-inked maps only make sense where print does not exist. That is why she has a job.
- Map-state continuity: the map stays on the TABLE. She never carries the unfinished sheet to the shelf. The shelf is storage; the work happens at the desk.
- p4 bug is the PAIRING, not the sheet: a finished map set beside a blank one reads as two finished sheets. Do not stage them as a matched pair.
- Handedness RIGHT, works left to right, wet line never smudges. Hands outside the crop, never hidden.
- The blank is the calmest thing in the room: no creature, no shape, no silhouette. Page 1 ends at the NOTICING.
