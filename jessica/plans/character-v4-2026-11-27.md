## What v4 deliberately keeps from v3

The face lines (eye colour, eye shape, nose, muzzle), the eight head-heights number, the
magenta-pink pendant as the only glow, eyes on the work rather than the lens, the whole-figure
framing sentence, and the fire-red hair with the ginger gone from the positive.

## What is still unproven in it

Whether dropping the studio name fixes proportions or just removes the only thing holding the
figure at cartoon scale, and whether "curvaceous ... full soft bust" buys shape without the
engine rounding the whole torso. Both are one roll each, not a verdict. v4 has never been
generated.
